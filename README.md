# Customer Churn Analysis | SQL, Excel, Python, Machine Learning & Power BI

## 1. Project Overview

Customer Churn Analysis is a data analytics and business intelligence project focused on analyzing customer attrition and identifying the reasons associated with customer churn. The project integrates SQL, Microsoft Excel, Python, Machine Learning, and Power BI to explore customer data, uncover churn-related patterns, and present meaningful business insights.

Customer churn is an important business metric that indicates the loss of customers over a specific period. Understanding churn patterns and the reasons behind customer departures helps organizations gain a clearer understanding of customer behavior and identify areas that require further investigation.

This project uses data analysis, predictive modeling, and interactive visualization to examine customer churn and communicate relevant findings through a Power BI dashboard.

## 2. Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

- Analyze customer data to understand churn patterns.
- Identify and examine the reasons associated with customer churn.
- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing to prepare data for analysis.
- Use SQL to query and analyze customer information.
- Apply Python for exploratory data analysis and machine learning.
- Develop a machine learning model to predict customer churn, if implemented in the workflow.
- Visualize churn-related metrics and findings through Power BI.
- Present analytical results in a clear and structured format.
- Support data-driven interpretation of customer attrition.

## 3. Business Problem

Customer churn occurs when customers discontinue their relationship with a company or stop using its products or services. Understanding why customers leave is important for evaluating customer behavior and identifying potential business challenges.

However, customer data may contain multiple attributes and patterns that are difficult to interpret through raw tables alone.

This project addresses the analytical challenge by examining customer information, studying the reasons associated with churn, and presenting the findings through visual reports. The objective is to make churn-related information easier to understand and help users identify patterns that may require further investigation.

## 7. Churn Reasons Analysis

An important component of the Power BI dashboard is the visualization of **Churn Reasons**.

This section focuses on examining the recorded reasons associated with customer departures and understanding how churn is distributed across the available reason categories.

The analysis can help answer questions such as:

- What reasons are recorded for customer churn?
- Which churn reasons account for a larger share of customer departures?
- How is churn distributed across the available reason categories?
- Which reasons appear more frequently in the dataset?
- What patterns can be observed in the recorded churn reasons?

Visualizing churn reasons makes the information easier to interpret and helps identify categories that may require additional investigation.

The findings should be interpreted according to the categories and values available in the dataset. The dashboard presents recorded churn reasons and does not, by itself, establish that a particular factor caused a customer to leave.
